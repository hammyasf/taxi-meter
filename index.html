Questions

I pay for a subscription. Is the fare my bill?

No. The fare is what the same use would cost on the API at list prices.

Does Taxi Meter send my logs anywhere?

No. It reads ~/.claude/projects on your Mac and has no network code.

Why is Taxi Meter's first launch slower?

It reads 30 days of logs once, with a progress bar, then only new lines.

How exact is the 5-hour window?

It is an estimate built from your local logs.

What if a model is missing from the prices?

The meter counts the calls it could not price. Add the rates to prices.json and restart.
